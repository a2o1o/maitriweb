What changes

Maitri's impact is visible in how girls imagine their future, access opportunities, and return to lead others.
Ambition

Wider horizons

Girls encounter role models, career stories, and peer examples that expand what they see as possible for themselves.

Access

Structured pathways

Scholarship guidance, internships, exposure opportunities, and career support help convert ambition into next steps.

Leadership

Girls return as guides

Maitri members become peer leads, mentors, content creators, and community builders for juniors following similar paths.

Methodology

What we measure and how

We track three distinct layers: inputs (sessions conducted, participation), outputs (scholarships, internships, jobs, leadership roles), and early signals (expression, confidence, attendance, and continuity).
