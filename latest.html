Debate finale · Update published 16 October 2026

Maitri Debate 2026: Finding the courage to speak

Weeks of research, preparation, and practice came together at the Navratri Community Meet, where four teams took part in the final rounds of Maitri's first debate initiative.

Kirti, Nidhi, Yukti, and Shreya explored shared responsibility for women's safety. Abhilasha, Saloni, Geetanjali, and Shashi examined the role of technology in gender-based violence.

The finale gave participants a platform to develop arguments, consider different viewpoints, and express their ideas in front of an audience. Guest judge Ms. Anubha Srivastava offered guidance on public speaking, perspective-building, and storytelling.
