Our purpose

Skills, opportunity, and a future of giving back

Our mission

Our mission is to give specific skills to underprivileged girls and boys to make them employable. And in this manner bring economic prosperity to their families.

Our vision

Our vision is to make SAIJAYA YOUTH INITIATIVE TRUST an organisation that has transformed and empowered the life of thousands of youth and put them on the path of learning and giving back to society.

About the trust

Saijaya Youth Initiative Trust supports initiatives that expand learning, leadership, and access for young people. Maitri is one of the youth-focused initiatives supported by the trust.
Education

Scholarship and continuity support

Supporting young people with education-linked costs that help them stay enrolled and continue their pathway.

Leadership

Youth-led responsibility

Encouraging young people to take ownership, lead peer groups, and contribute to community growth.

Community

Opportunity access

Enabling mentoring, exposure, career guidance, and networks that support critical life transitions.

Maitri and Saijaya

Maitri's work with girls from Delhi government and low-income school communities is supported through Saijaya Youth Initiative Trust. Contributions help enable scholarships, mentoring, exposure opportunities, leadership programs, and school-to-work transition support.
